# Museum prologue

Original built-in imagegen asset: `public/cinematics/prologue-museum.png`.
Fictional gallery inspired by the National Museum of Korea, not a documentary representation.

## Prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Original Korean fantasy mobile game prologue illustration, portrait 2:3. Interior of a modern Korean national museum exhibition hall, evocative of the National Museum of Korea but a fictional gallery, quiet subdued blue-gray stone walls, tasteful museum spotlights. A single young adult visitor in modern casual clothes and small backpack, seen from behind in three-quarter view at center-left, looking closely at an ancient Korean thread-bound book displayed open inside a clearly visible tall GLASS DISPLAY CASE at center-right. The book emits a very faint warm gold magical glow, reflected subtly in the glass. Person and illuminated book clearly readable together in upper middle 25%-60% of frame; bottom 30% quiet dim museum floor kept uncluttered for dialogue overlay. Beautiful hand-painted anime game cinematic art, restrained mystery, believable anatomy, atmospheric depth, warm highlights on cool interior. No crowds, no combat, no medieval clothing on visitor. NO text, no title, no labels, no speech bubbles, no UI, no watermark.

The museum scene appears first both on initial play and replay. Dialogue is rendered as accessible HTML over the illustration. Scene titles, location labels and speaker labels are removed. Skip stays at top right. New players must supply a valid nickname before skipping the remainder; existing profiles continue immediately. No voice is added.
