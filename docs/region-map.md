## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create an original horizontal scrolling region-selection world map for a Korean historical mobile defense RPG. Wide panoramic 3:1 composition. Hand-painted illustrated aerial oblique view, detailed lush mountain ridges, pine forests, winding blue rivers, mist and ancient Korean architecture, soft jade teal and parchment gold palette. One continuous landscape, not separate panels. Four focal regions distributed left to right at x=14%,38%,64%,88%: at 14% y=52% a rugged mountain fortress representing Yodong; at 38% y=64% a larger Korean walled city on green plains representing Pyongyang; at 64% y=46% a broad winding river crossing representing Salsu; at 88% y=58% an imposing imperial army encampment with red banners representing the final battle. Small scenic architecture not huge buildings. Gentle connected paths across valleys, mountains behind, water and woods foreground. Rich premium mobile game map illustration, readable navigable geography with ample open space around four landmarks for interactive markers. Absolutely NO text, letters, labels, numbers, buttons, UI, character portraits, or watermark. Not a real geographic map. Do not copy any existing game.

## Rules

- Scroll wheel, touch swipe, keyboard arrows, and navigation buttons move horizontally.
- Yodong 1–3, Pyongyang 4–6, Salsu 7–9, final battle 10.
- Normal stages retain sequential unlock and saved progress.
- Hard mode remains disabled even after stage 10. Before completion its label explains the prerequisite; after completion it says preparation in progress. No hard gameplay is implemented yet.
